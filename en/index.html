How to price automation maintenance

A monthly retainer should reflect the work that actually needs to be done—not just the number of workflows. Estimate routine checks, likely incidents, coordination time, and a realistic reserve. Then calculate the labor price from your own cost and target gross margin.

Be explicit about the workflows covered, included hours, support hours, response targets, third-party costs, and changes outside the plan. A monthly maintenance fee does not automatically include 24/7 availability or new feature development.
